WHAT TO JUDGE

Earth first.
Fantasy in the telling.

Does each material read clearly? Does the grain feel painted rather than photographic? Do slope changes feel inevitable?

The Holler compendium’s fine marks and restrained ink inform the direction. This study interprets that restraint as layered pigment and terrain-following marks, rather than stamped symbols or heavy outlines.

This is a procedural visual prototype, not an erosion or material-transport simulation. No terrain, project format, tools or saved worlds in the editor have changed. Moss, peat, heath, marsh, gravel, shale and sandstone are deferred; everyday Dirt is covered by Loam.
