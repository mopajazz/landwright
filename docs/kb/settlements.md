# Settlements brush

1. Choose **Settlements** in the toolbox or press **T**.
2. Pick **Hamlet**, **Village**, **Town**, **Abbey**, or **Castle** from the illustrated swatches. Use **W/S** or **Shift + mouse wheel** to resize the search area.
3. Move over the map to preview the plan. Click to place it, or drag to reposition the preview and release to place. **Escape** cancels the gesture.

Landwright searches the brush area for connected dry ground and routes lanes through gentle slopes. Hamlets group a few roofs, villages favor a main lane, towns leave room for a square, abbeys group around a court, and castles favor higher ground for a keep. A failed preview explains how to adjust the site or brush size. Placement works in both editing views and leaves ground heights and forest density intact.

A placement is one undo action. The project stores the built plan so subsequent sculpting, save and reload preserve it. Older settlement records still load. Settle on another site by removing the original through **Paint → Shift erase target → Settlements**, then placing again. The Settlements layer controls visibility and locks. Visible places appear in the Map PNG. The Heightmap PNG contains ground elevations.
